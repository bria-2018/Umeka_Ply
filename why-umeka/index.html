Our Manufacturing Process

Step-by-step engineering at our Chopadva, Kutch manufacturing facility.

Step 1

Raw Timber & Peeling

Selection of dense timber logs, peeled into uniform veneer sheets and dried to controlled moisture levels.

Step 2

Adhesive & Cross-Lamination

Fortified synthetic bonding resin applied across veneers, stacked with alternating 90° grain orientations.

Step 3

High-Tonnage Hydraulic Press

Hot-pressed under monitored heat and tonnage to ensure complete glue polymerization and void-free core bonding.

Step 4

Dual-Side Calibration Sanding

Panels pass through wide-belt calibrating sanders, eliminating thickness variations and creating a glass-flat face.

Step 5

Anti-Borer & Termite Infusion

Chemical preservative treatment infused to safeguard panel cores against insect infestation.

Step 6

Batch Inspection & QC

Every batch is checked for squareness, thickness tolerance, surface finish, and edge integrity before dispatch.
