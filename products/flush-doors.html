Doors face constant swinging forces and atmospheric temperature swings. Umeka doors are built for lasting dimensional stability.

Kiln-Seasoned Hardwood Frame

Perimeter framing stiles and rails are kiln-seasoned to controlled moisture levels, preventing seasonal warping, twisting, or corner drop.

Solid Internal Core Filler

Dense solid or semi-solid timber infill gives the door a substantial, premium acoustic weight with reliable lock, latch, and closer screw holding.

Smooth Calibrated Skin

Ultra-flat surface faces allow seamless pressing of 1mm laminates, acrylic sheets, or thin decorative veneers with zero telegraphing ripples.
