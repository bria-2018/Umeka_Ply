Maximum bending resistance for tables & desks.

Structural Rigidity

Engineered for Long Horizontal Spans

Where standard panels might deflect or bend under continuous load, Umeka Block Board maintains flat structural stability.

High Longitudinal Stiffness

The solid timber battens run parallel along the board length, offering superior resistance against bending across expansive table spans and open shelves.

Lightweight Handling

Because the core is solid seasoned wood rather than adhesive-saturated dense veneer plies, it remains significantly lighter and easier to transport and assemble.

Solid Edge Holding

Direct solid timber battens ensure wood screws, dowels, and edge hinges bite firmly without splitting or leaving hollow voids.
