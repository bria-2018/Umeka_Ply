Engineering Selection Guide

Comparative Material Matrix

How to specify between Calibrated Plywood, Block Board, and Flush Doors based on structural requirements.

Product Category Core Construction Key Strength Moisture Tolerance Standard Thicknesses Primary Applications
Calibrated Plywood Alternating cross-grain hardwood veneers bonded under high hydraulic pressure Uniform thickness across panel, high screw retention, flat laminate pressing High (MR, BWR, BWP marine options available) 6mm, 9mm, 12mm, 16mm, 19mm, 25mm Kitchens, wardrobes, TV units, cabinetry, wall panelling
Block Board Solid seasoned timber battens with balanced surface veneer crossbands Longitudinal rigidity, zero sagging over long horizontal spans, lightweight Moderate to High (Interior MR & Exterior BWR) 19mm, 25mm Tabletops, wide bookshelves, vertical partitions, long counters
Flush Doors Solid seasoned timber frame & batten core with dual-side calibrated skins Acoustic damping, high slam resistance, flat warp-free swing Moisture resistant bonded 30mm, 32mm, 35mm, 38mm Bedrooms, main entrance shutters, office cabins, hotel rooms
