    Common questions

    What is the difference between plywood and block board?

    Plywood is made of thin veneer layers glued together with the grain of each layer turned 90° to the next. Block board has a core of solid wood strips with veneer on both faces. Plywood is the usual choice for cabinets and furniture carcasses. Block board suits wide, flat panels such as tabletops and shelves.

    What does "calibrated" plywood mean?

    Calibrated plywood is sanded to a precise, uniform thickness. That makes shutters, shelves and joints fit cleanly, and cuts down on shimming and rework on site. Umeka's thickness tolerance will be published here once confirmed.

    Which plywood is right for a kitchen?

    Kitchens see steam, spills and heat, so choose a grade made for moisture or water resistance. In Indian standards, IS 303 covers general-purpose plywood and IS 710 covers marine-grade plywood. The exact Umeka grades will be listed on each product page.
