## Read this first: five things that need your decision

**1. Certifications and warranty cannot be "same as Margo".**
You asked for these to match margoply.com. I reviewed the Margo site: it lists **no BIS/ISI, E-grade or test certificates at all**. For Umeka:
- Any certificate must be issued to Umeka or the Umeka manufacturing unit.
- "Virokill" is a third-party technology. Confirm Umeka holds its own licence before using the name.
- A lifetime warranty is a legal promise. Write the terms first, then publish.
- The site has four empty "document slots" (certificates, test reports, warranty, emissions) that only go live when a real file is uploaded.

**2. Your office is in the same estate and near the same landmark as Margo's** (Highway Estate, opposite Kiran Motors, Sarkhej-Sanand Road), and the phone numbers are close (…8788 vs …8771). Customers may confuse the two. Mitigation: use "Umeka" with a clear unit number in every listing, create a separate Google Business Profile, and never use "Margo" in page copy, meta keywords, alt text or ad keywords.

**3. Manufacturing unit.** I used the Chopadva (Bhachau, Kutch) address as Umeka's manufacturing unit and the line "made in Kutch, Gujarat". **[VERIFY]** whether this is Umeka's own unit or a partner unit. That changes how "we make" language should be worded.

"303" and "710" are Indian Standard numbers, not Margo's, but the product-line names are. Umeka needs its own product-line and grade names. **[VERIFY: product line names, grades, thicknesses, sizes]**

**5. Photography.** I could not open Envato or the site you called "pixcel.com" (I couldn't identify it; if you meant Pexels or Pixabay, that works). ### Industry conventions Umeka should meet
- Product hierarchy: category → product line → grade → specs and sizes.
- Applications by room or use.
- Visible certificates, warranty terms and test information.
- Separate paths for homeowners, professionals and dealers.
- Catalogue download, WhatsApp, phone and one short enquiry form.
- Guides that explain grades (MR, BWR, BWP) in plain language.

### Where Umeka can stand apart
1. **Explain the material.** An interactive "Built layer by layer" explorer, which no reviewed site offers.
2. **Proof before promise.** Every claim links to a document.
3. **Fewer clicks.** Three products, one requirement finder, one form.
4. **Mobile-first for the Indian buyer.** Sticky Call / WhatsApp / Enquire bar, large tap targets, light pages.
5. **A distinct look.** Warm wood, deep green and a serif headline, in contrast to the green-and-white or blue-and-white palettes most competitors use.

### 4.2 Calibrated Plywood (category page)
**H1:** Calibrated plywood for furniture, kitchens and interiors
**Intro:** Calibrated plywood is sanded to a uniform thickness, so panels fit, joints close and finishes sit flat. It is the everyday choice for wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, TV units, wall panelling and modular furniture.

**Why it matters**
- Even thickness reduces shimming, gaps and rework on site.
- Layers are glued cross-grain, which keeps panels flat and stable.
- Available in grades for interior and moisture-prone areas. **[VERIFY: grade names, thicknesses, sizes, standards]**

**Best for:** Wardrobes · Kitchen cabinets · Beds and storage · TV units · Wall panelling · Office furniture
**CTAs:** *View product lines* · *Download catalogue* **[VERIFY: catalogue file]** · *Talk to an expert*

### 4.3 Block Board (category page)
**H1:** Block board for tabletops, shelves and partitions
**Intro:** Block board has a core of solid wood strips between veneer faces. That gives a flat, rigid panel that stays light for its size, a practical choice for wide surfaces.

**Best for:** Tabletops · Long shelves · Partitions · Doors and door frames · Office desks
**Detail to add:** core wood species, strip width, face veneer, thicknesses, sizes, grade. **[VERIFY]**

### 4.4 Flush Doors (category page)
**H1:** Flush doors for bedrooms, offices and cabins
**Intro:** A flush door has a flat, smooth face on both sides, over a solid frame and core. It is ready to be finished in laminate, veneer or paint to suit the room.

**Best for:** Bedrooms · Bathrooms *(only if a water-resistant variant is offered [VERIFY])* · Offices · Hotels and hospitality · Apartments
**Detail to add:** sizes, thicknesses, core, skin materials, frame, finishes. **[VERIFY]**

## Stage 5: Design direction and visual recommendations

**Concept: "The cross-section."** The brand is built from the thing itself: layers of wood. The hero, the explorer and the product panels all draw from plywood's edge and grain.

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Ivory `#F7F4EE` · Sand `#E7DDCB` · Walnut `#5C3F2A` · Charcoal `#26251F` · Deep green `#1F3A31` · Bronze `#A9803F` (small accents only) |
| **Headlines** | **Noto Serif Display**, light weights, large sizes, tight tracking. The hero uses roman + italic for rhythm. |
| **Body** | **Inter**, 17 px base, 1.6 line height, lines under 70 characters. |
| **Layout** | Full-width sections with a content cap of 1680 px so ultra-wide screens do not stretch. Asymmetric product grid on desktop, single column on mobile. |
| **Shape** | Nearly square corners (2 px). Architectural, not app-like. |
| **Motion** | One page-load moment (the plywood layers extend across the hero) and one scroll moment (the layers open once when the explorer appears). Everything else responds to the user only. Reduced-motion is respected. |
| **Photography** | Warm, natural light, real Indian interiors, close-up grain and cross-sections, door details, workshop craft. No smiling-family stock, no other brand's logos. |
| **Imagery slots** | Hero band (cross-section photo can sit behind it later), three product panels (1600×1200), applications (1600×1000), professionals (1200×800). |
| **Avoid** | Neon, glow effects, glassmorphism, heavy gradients, sci-fi visuals. |
| **Logo** | Use your supplied logo. The prototype uses a temporary wordmark. Provide an SVG for the header and footer. |

**Responsive plan:** design at 360 px first, then 600, 900, 1200 and 1680. Minimum tap target 44 px. Product panels stack on mobile; the layer explorer puts the visual first, then controls.

## Stage 7: Suggested technology stack

**Recommended: Next.js + headless CMS**

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Front end | **Next.js (React), TypeScript** | Fast static pages, excellent SEO, image optimisation, and a strong ecosystem. Suits animations and the interactive explorer. |
| Styling | **Tailwind CSS** with design tokens | Consistent spacing and colour, small CSS output. |
| Motion | CSS first, **Motion (Framer Motion)** only where needed | Keeps JavaScript light for mobile devices. |
| CMS | **Sanity** (or Strapi if you want self-hosting) | Non-technical staff can add products, specs, PDFs, FAQs and guides. A structured product schema fits catalogue data. |
| Forms | Next.js server action → email service + Google Sheets API + WhatsApp alert | Meets your requirement of email, WhatsApp and Sheet. Add Cloudflare Turnstile or reCAPTCHA against spam. |
| Hosting | **Vercel** (or Netlify) with a CDN; Cloudflare in front for DNS and security | Global CDN, automatic HTTPS. Confirm an India or nearby region for speed. |
| Analytics | GA4 + Search Console; Meta Pixel only if you run ads | Track form submissions, WhatsApp clicks and calls. |
| Fonts | Self-host Noto Serif Display and Inter via `next/font` | Faster and avoids layout shift. |

**Why not WordPress?** WordPress is workable and cheaper to start with, but it usually needs more plugins for the same result, and speed and security take more maintenance. If your developer is stronger in WordPress, a lightweight custom theme with ACF for product data is an acceptable second choice.

**Security:** HTTPS everywhere, form validation on the server, rate limiting, secrets in environment variables, regular dependency updates, and a privacy policy that explains how enquiry data is used.

## Stage 8: SEO and performance recommendations

### SEO
- **Primary topics:** calibrated plywood, block board, flush doors, plywood manufacturer in Gujarat, plywood dealer enquiry. Add city terms (Ahmedabad, Gandhidham, Kutch) in natural copy and on the contact and dealer pages.
- **One H1 per page**, logical H2/H3 structure, descriptive image alt text.
- **Schema:** Organization, LocalBusiness (office), Product, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage. The prototype already includes Organization.
- **Metadata:** unique title (under 60 characters) and description (under 155) for every page, plus Open Graph images.
- **Content:** publish the guides in Stage 4.10. They target the questions buyers actually search.
- **Local:** create a Google Business Profile for the Ahmedabad office; add photos, hours and the WhatsApp number.
- **Internal links:** product ↔ application ↔ guide pages.
- **Avoid:** using "Margo" anywhere, keyword stuffing, duplicate descriptions.

### Performance targets
- Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s on a mid-range phone on 4G.
- Layout shift under 0.1; interaction delay under 200 ms.
- Images in AVIF/WebP with responsive sizes; lazy-load below the fold.
- Under 200 KB of JavaScript on the homepage, and load the layer explorer only when it appears.
- Prefer CSS animation; no autoplay video with sound; compressed short loops only if used.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA colour contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support.
