Our Heritage

A Quarter Century of Timber Craft

The journey behind Umeka began in 1998 as Jyoti Enterprise, working closely with timber merchants, carpenters, and master craftsmen. Over two and a half decades on factory floors and construction sites, we observed the recurring challenge in the plywood industry: uneven board thickness that leads to warped wardrobe doors, bubbling veneers, and endless shimming on site.

Umeka was founded to solve this definitively. By pairing deep timber knowledge with state-of-the-art dual-head wide-belt calibration machinery in Kutch, Gujarat, we produce calibrated plywood, solid batten block boards, and flush doors that meet the exacting standards of contemporary modular joinery.

Precision Infrastructure

The Kutch Manufacturing Plant

Located in Chopadva, Bhachau, Gandhidham, our plant combines large-capacity processing with meticulous quality control.

Hydraulic Press
01

Hydraulic Hot Pressing

Heavy hydraulic multi-daylight presses cure synthetic phenol formaldehyde resins deep into core veneer cells under strict heat and pressure curves.

Dual Calibration
02

Wide-Belt Calibration

Dual-head calibrating sanders smooth both board faces simultaneously, eliminating thickness drift across every square foot of the panel.

Kiln Seasoning
03

Kiln Seasoning Chambers

Timber battens and core veneers undergo controlled dehumidification to balance moisture content and prevent post-installation board warp.
