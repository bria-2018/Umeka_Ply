What Makes Kitchen Woodwork Demanding?

A breakdown of why Indian kitchens require calibrated moisture-proof panels.

Under-Sink Moisture & Steam

Moisture from dishwashing and cooking steam can cause cheap commercial ply to swell. Umeka's fortified adhesive bonding prevents glue line failure.

Heavy Drawer Channel Cycling

Pots, pans, and cutlery add substantial load to drawer slides. High-density core veneers ensure screws hold tightly without stripping.

Precision Hinge Alignment

Even a 1mm variation in shutter thickness ruins zero-gap modular alignments. Dual-side calibration ensures flush, straight gaps.
